# AZAAD EXECUTIVE ANALYTICS

## Scope

Central management dashboard over verified operational aggregates.

### KPIs
- Appointments and completion rate
- No-shows
- Revenue
- Expenses
- Operating result
- Outstanding balances
- Doctor/service performance
- Workforce indicators
- Marketing indicators
- Security/exception indicators

### Time ranges
- Today
- This week
- This month
- This year
- Previous-period comparison

### AI layer
AI explains trends and proposes operational priorities. It must show the supporting metric and period for each recommendation.

### Financial boundary
Database-calculated financial totals are authoritative. AI cannot alter accounting records, refunds, write-offs, prices, commissions, payroll or payments automatically.

### Privacy
Executive views use minimum-necessary aggregates. Patient-level clinical details remain restricted to authorized clinical roles.

### Reliability
The dashboard must render deterministic metrics without an external AI provider. AI enhancement is optional and has a clear fallback.
