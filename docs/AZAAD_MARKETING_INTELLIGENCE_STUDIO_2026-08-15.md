# AZAAD MARKETING INTELLIGENCE STUDIO

## Channels

- Instagram
- Facebook
- TikTok
- LinkedIn
- Website

## Media-first composer

The production UI must remain a large, fast workspace supporting:

- Image upload/preview.
- Video upload/preview.
- Caption and description.
- Per-channel variants.
- Drafts.
- Scheduled.
- Published history.
- Edit.
- Archive/delete.
- Search/filter.
- CTA/link.
- Hashtags.
- Campaign association.
- Human approval before publishing.

## AI behavior

AI adapts content to the channel:

- Instagram: visual caption, carousel/reel concept and hashtags.
- Facebook: community/offer-oriented copy.
- TikTok: hook, short script and concise caption.
- LinkedIn: professional education, expertise and employer branding.
- Website: SEO article, landing page and offer copy.

The AI must not claim clinical outcomes, fabricate testimonials, or invent medical evidence. Healthcare marketing must be reviewed by an authorized human before publication.

## Free-first rule

Core drafting, media management and draft history work without paid social APIs. Platform APIs are optional adapters. If a platform integration is unavailable, the content remains available for manual publishing.

## Analytics

Track, where data is legally and technically available:

- Reach/impressions.
- Engagement.
- Clicks.
- Leads.
- Bookings attributed to campaigns.
- Conversion rate.
- Cost when paid campaigns exist.

Do not infer patient identity from marketing analytics. Use campaign-level or de-identified aggregates.
